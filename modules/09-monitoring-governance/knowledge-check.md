# Knowledge Check

## Questions

1. What questions can usage metrics help answer?
2. What are common causes of refresh failure?
3. Why should tenant settings be reviewed as part of governance?
4. What is the role of a gateway in refresh operations?
5. Why are activity logs and audit logs marked **Verify for Gov**?
6. Why might the admin monitoring workspace be unavailable in a customer environment?
7. What does the capacity metrics app help diagnose?
8. Why are Purview and DLP marked **Verify for Gov**?
9. What should be included in an operations runbook?
10. Why should operational content reference the PBIP source location?

## Answer key

1. Usage metrics help identify adoption, active users, report views, consumption trends, and candidates for improvement or retirement.
2. Credentials, gateway issues, source downtime, privacy settings, schema changes, unsupported connectors, or query failures can cause refresh failures.
3. Tenant settings control sharing, export, certification, external users, and other risk-sensitive behaviors.
4. A gateway connects Power BI Service to on-premises or network-restricted data sources.
5. They require admin permissions, audit configuration, and cloud support that must be validated.
6. Availability can vary by cloud, tenant configuration, role, and Microsoft rollout status.
7. It helps diagnose capacity workload pressure, refresh impact, interactive performance, and throttling symptoms.
8. They depend on M365/Purview cloud support, licensing, label policies, tenant settings, and compliance configuration.
9. Owners, sources, refresh schedule, access model, monitoring cadence, incident response, escalation paths, and validation notes.
10. PBIP is the source-controlled artifact used for change review and reproducible maintenance.
