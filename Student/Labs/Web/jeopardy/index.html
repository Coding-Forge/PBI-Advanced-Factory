Jeopardy Review Boards

Classroom-ready Jeopardy games for reviewing what each module covered. Project a board, split learners into teams, click a dollar tile to reveal a clue, reveal the answer, then award points to the team that answered correctly. Scores and used tiles are saved per board in the browser, so a board can be reset and replayed.
